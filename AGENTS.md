# Andrew Fonseka’s portfolio

The site helps agency and in-house hiring managers decide whether to interview Andrew for a paid media role. Agency roles managing multiple accounts come first; in-house paid media roles are a secondary target, so the contact and About copy speak to both. The primary action is an email to Andrew. Production is `andrewfonseka.com`, built by Vercel project `andrew-fonseka-portfolio` from `main` in [the GitHub repository](https://github.com/andrewfon24/Andrew-Fonseka-Portfolio).

## Editorial decisions (24 November 2026, spec v2.0)

- This repository is public. Only claims in `docs/career-facts.md` may appear in source, pages, metadata or PDFs. The full claims register stays outside the repo.
- Freelance work (Saudi university, DIMO Academy) and the B2B apparel account are removed. `/case-studies/university/` and `/case-studies/dats/` redirect to `/#results` in `vercel.json`.
- Homepage order: hero, proof strip, Results (Aegon, then Kites Global: Pan Asia Bank, Godrej, Petmart), Approach, About, Toolkit, References, contact. Andrew removed the three-problems section from the homepage (24 Nov 2026). Navigation: Results, Approach, About, References, Contact.
- Hero: eyebrow “Andrew Fonseka, MBA · Paid Media Specialist”, headline “Most ad reports stop at the click. Mine start there.”, role-first copy in Andrew’s voice, “Contact me” and “View résumé (PDF)”, then “Open to paid media roles.” No location, availability or work authorization anywhere on the site.
- Case pages carry no contact block in the facts panel; the shared footer handles contact. The footer bar spans the full page width, like the navbar.
- Every case follows Context, My role, What we did, Outcome with an evidence note, What this doesn’t show, Next time. Figures always carry their period and scope.
- The campaign builder demo is synthetic and scripted: no AI service, no network calls, human approval before an illustrative CSV.
- Vercel Analytics stays as it was; add no new tracking until Andrew sets it up.
- Glass navbar with a square headshot before the name. Pill buttons and links. Navy #0c2037 and blue #2164a9, Archivo headings, Inter body.
- Update this guide or docs/career-facts.md in the same commit as a change to an approved decision or claim.
